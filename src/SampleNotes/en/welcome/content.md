# Welcome to ScreenPinNotes

Sticky notes that stay on your screen.

- **Double-click the body**: write (`Esc` to stop)
- **Drag the title bar**: move
- **Double-click the title bar**: collapse
- **Right-click**: color, icon, reminder, and more

Create a note with `Ctrl+Alt+N` or from the tray icon.

For the full guide and the Markdown syntax, open "Usage Help" and "Markdown Syntax List" from **Hidden notes** in the tray menu.

Feel free to delete this note.
